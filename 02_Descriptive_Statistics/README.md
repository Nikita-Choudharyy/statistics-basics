# 📊 02 - Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive Statistics is one of the most important foundations of **Data Science and Machine Learning**.

It helps us understand and summarize a dataset before applying statistical analysis or building a Machine Learning model.

Instead of looking at every individual observation, Descriptive Statistics allows us to answer questions such as:

- 📍 Where is the data centered?
- 📏 How much does the data vary?
- 📈 How is the data distributed?
- 🚨 Are there extreme or unusual values?
- 🔍 Where do observations lie within the dataset?

This module focuses on the most important statistical measures used to summarize and understand numerical data.

## 🧠 Learning Approach

The purpose of this module is not simply to learn:

```text
Mean = Average
Median = Middle
Mode = Most Frequent
```

Instead, the goal is to develop the habit of asking:

> **"What does this statistic tell me about my data?"**

For every statistical measure, try to understand:

```text
What does it measure?
        ↓
Why do we need it?
        ↓
How is it calculated?
        ↓
How does it behave with outliers?
        ↓
How do I interpret it?
        ↓
Where is it used in Data Science?
        ↓
How can it help Machine Learning?
```
